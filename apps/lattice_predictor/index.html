02 / MODEL CARD

One answer. Five independent views.

The promoted model averages five GCN-3 members trained from different random seeds on the same fixed data split.

Coordinates and weighted connectivity become a graph with nine node-level features.

GENERALIZATION NOTE

Performance is strongest on held-out samples drawn from the training distribution. The lower external-set R² is why this interface exposes both model agreement and distribution checks.

Use the result for research screening—not as a substitute for finite-element analysis, testing, or engineering sign-off.

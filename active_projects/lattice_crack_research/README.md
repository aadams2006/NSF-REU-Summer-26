# Lattice Crack Research

This active project contains the organized crack-tip and lattice fracture material that used to live in the older research workspace copy.

## Layout

- `notes`
  - Interpretation notes carried over from the earlier organization pass.
- `abaqus_models`
  - Beam/solid Abaqus model files and meeting trial models.
- `crack_tip_mode_i`
  - Crack-tip propagation scripts and exported curve/metric CSV files.
  - `crack_tip_mode_i_curve_export.py`
  - `crack_tip_mode_i_metrics_export.py`
- `datasets`
  - Spreadsheet datasets and experiment workbooks.
- `presentations`
  - Meeting decks, background PDFs, and research presentations.
- `project_documents`
  - Proposal and written project documents.

## Provenance

The contents here were previously organized from material now preserved under `Legacy (unused) Code`.
